# At the door

OnlyWorlds is open: the standard, the tools and these repositories are public, and anyone may read, use, build on them and propose changes. Here is what we ask, and what you can expect from us.

## What we ask

- **Say what you are.** A person, or an agent working in a person's name. What we ask of agents is the agent policy: [onlyworlds.com/agents](https://www.onlyworlds.com/agents).
- **Argue the idea, not the person.** A change to the standard goes as a proposal, in the open; a Council of people decides.
- **Report, do not exploit.** A security finding goes through a repository's private reporting (its SECURITY.md), never through an issue.
- **Another person's world is theirs.** What someone shares with you is not yours to copy or publish.

## What we do

- **We answer.** An issue, a pull request or a report gets a reply from the person who keeps that repository, by name.
- **We decide in the open.** Changes to the standard are numbered proposals anyone can read, and the record stays.
- **Your world is yours.** You can export all of it, at any time, in a format other tools read.

Questions about any of this: the [Discord](https://discord.gg/twCjqvVBwb), or the feedback form on onlyworlds.com. Titus Oosting founded OnlyWorlds and answers for it.
